About

Dr. Cecilee ("Ceci") Hayes is a Russian Blue Nebelung and the devoted companion of Erica Hayes, Managing Librarian for Digital Scholarship,at Falvey Library. A thoughtful and contemplative researcher, Ceci's work investigates the intersections of human behavior,environmental enrichment, and feline well-being.

Her current research examines the relationship between laptop use, desk configurations, and lap availability, with a particular interest in how human productivity can be improved through strategic feline intervention. She is also engaged in a long-term investigation of cardboard boxes, exploring questions of occupancy, comfort, fit,and existential meaning.

Research Interests

Current areas of research include:

  • Laptop Use, Desk Configurations, and Lap Availability
  • The Existential Meaning of Boxes
  • Catnip and Catnip-Adjacent Objects
  • Evidence-Based Cuddling Practices
  • Human Training and Reinforcement Mechanisms
  • Keyboard Occupation as a Form of Scholarly Intervention
  • Advanced Sunbeam Utilization for Napping
  • Treat Acquisition Strategies
  • Window-Based Wildlife Observation
